abstract: 'Integrating underactuated anthropomorphic hands into complex teleoperation and telepresence systems often requires bridging heterogeneous middleware ecosystems and developing robotic control modules. In this paper, we present a modular software framework that orchestrates upper-body teleoperation and control with underactuated hands and complementary vision systems. The architecture seamlessly coexists across ROS 2 and YARP, leveraging the rich ROS 2 ecosystem alongside established low-level YARP devices. As a core contribution, we introduce a generic control system featuring joint-coupling solvers and modular building blocks applicable to diverse robotic platforms. We integrate the low-cost Dextra underactuated hand and validate the architecture through both simulated (Gazebo) and real-world experiments on the TEO humanoid robot and ABB GoFa collaborative manipulators. Furthermore, we demonstrate the framework''s versatility beyond teleoperation by executing an automated Rubik''s cube solving task. All source code has been open-sourced to provide a foundation for future developments.'
