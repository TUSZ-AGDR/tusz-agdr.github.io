abstract: 'We extend our previously published hardware- and middleware-agnostic generic teleoperation architecture to integrate the Dobot X-Trainer input controllers, adding haptic feedback to the overall control scheme. YARP and ROS 2 middleware frameworks are leveraged to enable seamless cross-middleware interoperability across diverse robotic platforms and their simulated environments and models, such as those evaluated in this work: the TEO humanoid, the ABB CRB 15000-5 "GoFa" industrial cobot, and Dobot collaborative arms. Haptic feedback is achieved by converting external force-torque measurements into motor command adjustments on the position-controlled X-Trainer input hardware. The architecture''s flexibility and performance are validated across simulated environments and physical systems.'
